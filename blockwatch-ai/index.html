How quickly can I open and connect an account?

Sign-up takes a couple of minutes. The free tier needs only an email — add a watch-only address or public node and you are reading data the same day.

Is Blockwatch governed, and where?

Add your legal entity, jurisdiction and regulatory status here.

How is my information protected?

Watchlists and notes are encrypted in transit and at rest. Blockwatch never takes custody of coins and never asks for private keys or seed phrases.

What are the real costs of access?

Observatory is free. Professional is $39 per seat per month, and Institutional is priced per organization. No setup fees, no metered charges.

Which systems and sources can I access?

Bitcoin base-layer data (blocks, mempool, nodes), miner statistics, exchange order books and macro indicators.

Do you support programmatic access?

Yes — Professional and Institutional include a REST API and webhooks.

Is there a sandbox account?

Yes — the free Observatory tier includes a read-only sandbox.

What support can I expect?

Observatory: docs and community. Professional: a dedicated senior support line. Institutional: a named account team.
